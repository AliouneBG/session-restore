Pick up where you left off

Session Restore quietly remembers what you have open. When your computer restarts, whether you meant it to or not, it offers to put everything back.

  • Your apps and windows, back on the right monitor, the right size.
  • Your browser tabs, in order, across Chrome, Edge and Firefox.
  • Nothing leaves this computer. There is no account and no server.

Connect your browsers

Tabs need a small browser extension. Open each browser, add the extension, and this page will tick it off as soon as it connects. You can skip any of them.

Private windows

Off unless you want it. When it is on, private and incognito tabs are encrypted on this computer, never uploaded, and erased automatically after a day.

Remember private windows
You can change this any time from the tray icon.

That is everything

Session Restore is running in the background now. There is nothing else to do: keep working, and it will be there when you sign back in.

  • Find it in the system tray, next to the clock.
  • Closed it? Search Start for Session Restore.
